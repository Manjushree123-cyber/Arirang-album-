About the Album

ARIRANG is BTS's tenth overall studio album, released on March 20, 2026 through BigHit Music. It marks the group's first full-length release in roughly six years and their first since all seven members — RM, Jin, Suga, J-Hope, Jimin, V, and Jung Kook — completed South Korea's mandatory military service.

The album takes its name from "Arirang," a traditional Korean folk song, and explores themes of identity, separation, reunion, fame, burnout, and cultural heritage. It spans a wide range of styles, from hip-hop to jersey club to rock-influenced tracks, reflecting the members' individual creative growth during their time apart.

The lead single, "SWIM," was the only track highlighted differently in the official tracklist reveal, signaling its role as the title track. The album was produced with a long list of collaborators, including Diplo, Ryan Tedder, Mike WiLL Made-It, Kevin Parker, Flume, and El Guincho.

ARIRANG was released alongside the announcement of a major world tour, the group's first since their hiatus, kicking off in Goyang, South Korea before continuing across Asia, Europe, and the Americas.
